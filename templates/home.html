Problem Statement & Solution

Multimodal Healthcare Triage Assistant for Government and Institutional Health Facilities

MediAssist — Triage is a human-in-the-loop triage assistant that helps government hospitals, primary health centres, public health camps, company clinics, industrial-estate health units and campus health centres summarize patient symptoms, uploaded reports, and basic visual inputs into a structured triage note for qualified review.

The solution is explicitly non-diagnostic: it organizes information, highlights urgency signals, and supports faster review, but it must not prescribe treatment or replace a qualified professional.

Capabilities

Live voice (Web Speech)Real-time speech-to-text via the browser Web Speech API — Hindi, English & regional languages. No audio recorded or uploaded.
Server OCRUpload lab/report photos. Server RapidOCR extracts text and parses structured test values and flags.
AI summarizationStructured triage note: chief complaints, timeline, red flags, missing info, follow-up questions.
Risk taggingEmergency / Urgent / Standard / Routine category plus a 0-100 score and urgency signal list.
Reviewer workflowRisk-prioritized queue for checkup requests, status workflow, expandable detail, referral preparation.
Referral prepBuild a referral note with priority, key info & transit precautions (non-prescriptive).

Privacy & Responsible AI Controls

  • Consent gate: No triage note is generated without explicit patient consent, recorded in the audit log.
  • Anonymization: Patients are referenced only by anonymous codes (PT-…). No names or phone numbers stored for triage sessions.
  • Append-only audit log: Consent, session creation, AI summary, OCR, review status, and referral actions are recorded with actor role and timestamp.
  • Non-diagnostic output: All outputs are advisory notes for a qualified human reviewer — never patient-facing instructions or diagnoses.
  • Consent-gated · Anonymized · Audit-logged · Role-based actions.
